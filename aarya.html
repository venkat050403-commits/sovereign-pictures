About the Story

In a kingdom shaped by power, loyalty and ancient secrets, two brothers stand on opposite sides of destiny. Alaric, the rightful elder heir, and Dracula, his younger brother, become connected to a conflict that threatens more than their own kingdom.

A hidden portal connects different worlds. A mysterious bracelet carries a secret. And a young princess named Aarya may hold the key to a destiny far greater than she could ever imagine.

As friendship, betrayal and forgotten truths collide, the boundaries between worlds begin to fall apart. The choices made in one kingdom could change them all.
