PROLOGUE

The Beginning of a Legend

Beyond the world we know, beyond the skies we see, there exist realms untouched by human imagination. Worlds with their own kingdoms. Their own legends. Their own secrets. And in one such world, hidden from the eyes of the universe, stood a kingdom unlike any other.

THE ROYAL KINGDOM

A Kingdom of Secrets

For generations, its people lived beneath the protection of their royal bloodline, unaware that the greatest threat to their future would not come from beyond their borders… But from a destiny already set in motion. Then, on a night when the stars seemed brighter than ever, a child was born within the royal palace. A princess.

A PRINCESS IS BORN

The Arrival of Aarya

Her arrival brought joy to the kingdom. Bells rang, celebrations filled the streets, and the people welcomed their future with hope. Her name was Aarya. Yet, beyond the celebrations, a secret was being prepared—a secret whose purpose even time itself had not yet revealed.

THE MYSTERIOUS BRACELET

A Secret of Protection

A mysterious bracelet, created to protect its bearer, carried a power unlike anything the kingdom had ever known. It would remain silent until the day it was needed. And when that day arrived, the fate of more than one world would rest upon the shoulders of a girl who had once known nothing beyond the walls of her home.

THE LEGEND BEGINS

A Fate Beyond Worlds

For some lives are born into royalty. Others are born into legend. But once in an age… A life is born to change the fate of worlds. This is the story of Aarya.
